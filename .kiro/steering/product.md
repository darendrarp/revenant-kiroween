# Product — REVENANT

REVENANT resurrects dead links. Link rot kills millions of pages every year; this
app gives anyone a one-paste way to check a URL, find its Wayback Machine
snapshot, and read it again — presented as a graveyard, because dead pages deserve
a proper haunting.

## Users

- **Readers** who hit a dead link in an article, citation, or bookmark.
- **Bloggers and maintainers** auditing their own pages for link rot.
- **Researchers** triaging lists of URLs.

## Core promise

Paste a URL → know in seconds whether it is alive → if dead, get its archived
version back with its life story. No account, no key, no fee.

## Non-goals

- No user accounts, no database, no server-side history.
- No AI summarization (the archive speaks for itself).
- No paywall or "pro" tier. The app is free and open source.
- Never fabricate archive data: if the Wayback Machine has no snapshot or cannot
  be reached, say so plainly.

## Tone

Spooky but dignified. Graveyard imagery, tombstones, "souls revived" — never
gory, never jokey about real loss. Copy is short, clear, and honest about what
the app can and cannot recover.
