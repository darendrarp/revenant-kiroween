# Demo video script — REVENANT (<3 minutes)

> Record a screen capture with voiceover (no face needed, no copyrighted music,
> no third-party trademarks beyond naming the Wayback Machine). Target length:
> **2:30–2:50**. Upload to YouTube as **public**. Keep the app's own audio off;
> narrate live or over the capture.

## Recording tips

- Use a clean browser profile, 1920×1080, dark mode (the app is dark-themed).
- Before recording, clear the graveyard (button "Bury them all") so shot 5
  shows exactly one revival.
- Pre-warm the demo: resurrect GeoCities once before recording so the archive
  responds fast on camera. If the archive is slow on the day, the loading
  state is part of the product — keep it, don't cut it.
- If a step fails live, say so and retry once on camera; a second take is
  cheaper than a faked demo.
- Captions: YouTube auto-captions are fine; check them once after upload.
- No background music needed. Silence + narration keeps it clean and avoids
  copyright issues.
